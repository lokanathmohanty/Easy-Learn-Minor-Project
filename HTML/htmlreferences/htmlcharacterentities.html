HTML - Character Entities

HTML entities are used to display special characters on a webpage that are reserved or have special meaning in HTML. An entity starts with & and ends with a semicolon (;).

For example, the less-than symbol < and greater-than symbol > must be written as entities in HTML.

Non-breaking Space

A non-breaking space prevents a line break between words. It is represented by &nbsp;.
