Power BI: Human Resource Analytics Dashboard

This dashboard is designed to analyze key HR metrics such as employee counts, attrition, salary distribution, and departmental performance.

Key questions addressed include:

  • Number of active employees by department
  • Total employees who left by gender and department
  • Monthly salary distribution by job role
  • Overall attrition rate
  • Total number of employees
  • Count of departments and sum of department ratings by minimum qualification

Steps to Build the Dashboard

  1. Load the dataset (e.g., Employee.xlsx) into Power BI Desktop.
  2. Use textbox to add a prominent dashboard title (e.g., "Human Resource Analytics Dashboard"), format font size and make it bold.
  3. Set report canvas background color for visual appeal.
  4. Create a pie chart showing count of active employees by department (legend: active employee status, values: department count).
  5. Use a stacked column chart to display sum of monthly salary by job role (X-axis: job role, Y-axis: monthly salary).
  6. Add a clustered bar chart to visualize employees who left by gender and department (Y-axis: gender, X-axis: employees left, legend: department).
  7. Create a calculated column for attrition rate using DAX: \[ \text{Attrition Rate} = \frac{\text{Employees Left}}{\text{Average Total Employees in Department}} \times 100 \]
  8. Use card visuals to display key metrics like attrition rate and total employees by dragging respective fields.
  9. Configure filters/slicers for criteria such as minimum qualification to dynamically segment data.
  10. Customize all visuals by adjusting size, colors, titles, legends, and enabling interactive drill-through for detailed insights.
  11. Enable drill actions to interactively explore data, for example clicking on a position shows details including attrition and employee count.

Benefits and Usage

  • Provides management with up-to-date insights on workforce composition and turnover.
  • Enables identification of departments or roles with high attrition for targeted interventions.
  • Supports HR decisions related to salary budgeting, recruitment, and talent management.
  • Promotes data-driven dialogue across organizational roles by tailoring dashboard views.
