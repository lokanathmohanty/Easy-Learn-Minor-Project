CSS justify-items Property

The CSS justify-items property is used to align grid items within their grid area along the inline (horizontal) direction. It defines how items are positioned inside their cells, controlling their alignment within the grid container.

Syntax

justify-items: legacy | normal | stretch | start | left | center | end | right | safe | unsafe | baseline | initial | inherit;

Property Values

  • legacy: Provides backward compatibility with older alignment rules. Default.
  • normal: Behaves like stretch in grid layouts.
  • stretch: Items stretch to fill the grid cell (default behavior if width is not set).
  • start: Aligns items at the start edge (left in LTR, right in RTL).
  • left: Aligns items to the left edge.
  • center: Aligns items in the center.
  • end: Aligns items at the end edge (right in LTR, left in RTL).
  • right: Aligns items to the right edge.
  • safe: If items overflow, alignment is reset to start for safety.
  • unsafe: Keeps alignment even if it causes overflow.
  • baseline: Aligns items along the text baseline.
  • initial: Sets the property to its default value.
  • inherit: Inherits the property from the parent element.

Examples

1. justify-items: normal

By default, items stretch to fill their grid cells. This behavior is equivalent to stretch.

Example: A grid with three items that stretch to fill their cells.

2. justify-items: stretch

Explicitly sets items to stretch across their grid cells if no width is defined.

3. justify-items: start

Aligns items at the start edge of their grid area. In left-to-right languages, items align left; in right-to-left, they align right.

4. justify-items: end

Aligns items at the end edge of their grid area. In left-to-right languages, items align right; in right-to-left, they align left.

5. justify-items: center

Aligns items at the center of their grid cells horizontally.

6. justify-items: left

Forces items to align with the left edge of their grid cell, regardless of text direction.

7. justify-items: right

Forces items to align with the right edge of their grid cell, regardless of text direction.

8. justify-items: baseline

Aligns items along the text baseline of their parent container, useful for consistent text alignment.

9. justify-items: legacy right

Ensures backward compatibility with older alignment systems, aligning items to the right.
