NumPy Certification Overview

W3Schools offers an online certification program for NumPy, designed for busy professionals who want to balance work, family, and career building.

The certification helps you document your skills, improve your career prospects, and study at your own pace with flexible exam scheduling.

More than 50,000 certificates have already been issued, trusted by top companies worldwide.

  • Those seeking to validate their competence in NumPy.

Group and Organizational Options

Educators, managers, or business owners can get certification packages for multiple users, ideal for schools or companies.

Example: Certification Exam Details

The NumPy certification exam tests your knowledge across multiple levels:

  • Intermediate level: Pass with at least 40% correct answers.
  • Advanced level: Pass with at least 75% correct answers.
  • Professional level: Pass with at least 90% correct answers.

The exam contains about 60 adaptive multiple-choice questions and must be completed within 60 minutes. You can attempt the exam up to three times without any expiration date.

Once passed, your certificate is available immediately and can be shared online using a unique link or QR code.
