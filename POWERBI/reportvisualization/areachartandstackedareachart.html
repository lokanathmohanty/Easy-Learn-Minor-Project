Power BI: Area Chart and Stacked Area Chart

An Area Chart displays the area between data points and the axis, typically used to compare field values over time or categories. The X-axis shows categorical values, while the Y-axis and Secondary Y-axis show numerical values.

A Stacked Area Chart combines multiple series by stacking their areas so that the total height represents cumulative values, useful for showing part-to-whole relationships over time or categories.

Creating an Area Chart

  • Load the dataset (Medical_History.xlsx) into Power BI Desktop.
  • Select the Area Chart visual in Report View.
  • Place EmployeeName in the X-axis and Sum of Stage_HbA1c in the Y-axis.
  • Add Sum of bl_gl_level to the Secondary Y-axis.
  • Use Small Multiples by adding Age to create multiple small area charts categorized by age.
  • Add fields like Heart_problem to Tooltips to show detailed info on hover.
  • Filter using Drill Through option for focused exploration (e.g., by blood pressure issues).

Creating a Stacked Area Chart

  • Select the Stacked Area Chart visual.
  • Place EmployeeName in the X-axis, and add bl_gl_level and BMI to the Y-axis.
  • Use the Legend to differentiate categories by color (note: cannot add legend if multiple fields are on Y-axis).
  • Use Small Multiples (e.g., add Diabetes field) to subdivide visuals.
  • Customize appearances such as font style, size, and color in the Format Visual pane.
  • Turn on Markers to indicate data points, Data Labels to show values, Total Labels for overall values, and Series Labels for series names.
  • Change background with images or colors, adjust transparency, and alter size and position under Properties.
  • Control visibility of titles, headers, icons, and tooltips for a cleaner look.
  • Add Alt Text for accessibility with screen readers.

Power BI: Area Chart and Stacked Area Chart

The Area Chart visualizes the region between data points and the axis, ideal for comparing field values over time or categories. The X-axis shows categorical values, while the Y and Secondary Y axes display numeric values.

The Stacked Area Chart stacks multiple series so their combined area represents cumulative totals, showing how each part contributes to the whole across categories.

How to Create an Area Chart

  • Import the dataset (e.g., Medical_History.xlsx) into Power BI Desktop.
  • Select the Area Chart visual in Report View.
  • Place EmployeeName on the X-axis and Sum of Stage_HbA1c on the Y-axis.
  • Add Sum of bl_gl_level to the Secondary Y-axis for comparison.
  • Use Small Multiples by adding Age to subdivide the chart by age groups.
  • Add fields (e.g., Heart_problem) to Tooltips to show details on hover.
  • Use Drill Through to filter data dynamically, such as by blood pressure problems.

How to Create a Stacked Area Chart

  • Select the Stacked Area Chart visual.
  • Place EmployeeName on the X-axis, and multiple fields like bl_gl_level and BMI on the Y-axis.
  • Use the Legend section to differentiate categories by color (limited with multiple Y-axis fields).
  • Apply Small Multiples (e.g., Diabetes) to create subdivided charts.
  • Customize font style, size, color, axis titles, and legend alignment in the Format Visual pane.
  • Enable Markers, Data Labels, Total Labels, and Series Labels for enhanced readability.
  • Adjust chart background with images, colors, and transparency for aesthetics.
  • Modify chart size, position, padding, and advanced settings under Properties.
  • Toggle visibility of titles, headers, and tooltips as desired.
  • Add Alt Text for accessibility to assist screen readers.
