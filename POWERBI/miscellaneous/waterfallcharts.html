Power BI: Waterfall Charts

Waterfall charts visualize increments and decrements in values over time or categories. The Y-axis shows changes in data values, while the X-axis shows periods like years. For example, they can display yearly increases or decreases in net sales. Colors highlight different changes: green for increases, red for decreases, blue for totals.

Benefits of Waterfall Charts

  • Effectively display sales production changes monthly or yearly.
  • Visualize attrition periods in employment.
  • Show cash inflows and outflows.
  • Display net sales revenue trends clearly.

How to Create a Waterfall Chart

  • Import a dataset such as waterfall model new.xlsx containing fields like Designation, Gross Sales, Name, Net Sales, Registration ID, and Year.
  • Select the Waterfall Chart visual from the Visualizations pane.
  • Drag Gross Sales to the Y-axis and Year to the Category section.
  • Add Net Sales to the Breakdown section to show increases and decreases per year.
  • Interpret the chart by green bars for rising sales, red bars for declining sales, and blue bars for totals.

Customizing the Waterfall Chart

  • X-axis: Change font size, color, and spacing of data labels under "Values" and "Layout".
  • Y-axis: Adjust min and max range. Optionally hide values and titles. Customize font and color for clarity.
  • Legend: Toggle visibility on/off. Change position (e.g., bottom right), font style, size, color, and add a legend title.
  • Columns: Customize colors of positive, negative, and total bars.
  • Breakdown: Modify breakdown intervals to control category grouping (e.g., set intervals to 2).
  • Data Labels: Enable to show values on bars. Change orientation and background color for better visibility.
