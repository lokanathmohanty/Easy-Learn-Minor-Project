CSS mix-blend-mode Property

The mix-blend-mode property in CSS determines how the content of an element blends with the content of its parent and the element's background.

Syntax

mix-blend-mode: normal | multiply | screen | overlay | darken | lighten | color-dodge | color-burn | difference | exclusion | hue | saturation | color | luminosity;

Property Values

  • normal – No blending occurs.
  • multiply – Darkens colors by multiplying background and foreground.
  • screen – Lightens colors by inverting, multiplying, and inverting again.
  • overlay – Combines multiply and screen, preserving highlights and shadows.
  • darken – Retains the darker color from overlapping elements.
  • lighten – Retains the lighter color from overlapping elements.
  • color-dodge – Brightens the background by decreasing color of the element.
  • color-burn – Darkens the background by increasing color of the element.
  • difference – Subtracts colors for high-contrast effects.
  • exclusion – Similar to difference, but with softer contrast.
  • hue – Preserves luminance and saturation, alters only hue.
  • saturation – Preserves luminance and hue, alters only saturation.
  • color – Combines hue and saturation with luminance.
  • luminosity – Preserves hue and saturation, alters luminance.
