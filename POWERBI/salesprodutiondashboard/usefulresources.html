Understanding DAX Aggregate Functions

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) includes a variety of aggregate functions to perform calculations on data. These functions help in summarizing data, such as finding totals, averages, minimum or maximum values from a column in a dataset.

Common Aggregate Functions

  • SUM: Returns the total of all values in a numeric column.
  • AVERAGE: Calculates the average of a numeric column.
  • MIN: Finds the smallest numeric value.
  • MAX: Finds the largest numeric value.
  • COUNT: Counts the number of non-empty values.

Usage in Power BI

To use aggregate functions in Power BI, create a new measure from the "Modeling" tab and use the DAX formula bar to define your function.

Example: Calculating Total Sales

Here is an example DAX expression to calculate the total sales from a Sales column:

total_sales = SUM(Orders[Sales])

Example: Finding the Highest Price

To get the maximum price from a Price column:

max_price = MAX(Products[Price])
