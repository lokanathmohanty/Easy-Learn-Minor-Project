NumPy Study Plan

The NumPy study plan helps you teach your students NumPy step-by-step. Creating a study plan for NumPy is easy. You can use a pre-built study plan or customize it according to your students' skill levels to ensure that everyone is challenged.

Save time with pre-built teacher materials and study plans, and easily organize your class with a timeline from the introduction of NumPy to the final exam.

W3Schools Academy

This study plan is a feature of W3Schools Academy, a platform with everything you need to teach coding all in one place. It offers a toolbox of features to help you succeed in teaching.

To access the study plan feature, an active subscription is required. Two subscription tiers are available:

  • Full Access ($5.99 / month per student)

Teacher Materials

W3Schools provides comprehensive NumPy training materials for your study plan, including:

  • W3Schools NumPy Tutorial
  • NumPy Exercises
  • NumPy Quiz
  • NumPy Challenges (Coding challenges)
  • NumPy Certification Exam (End of Pathway Exam)

Study Plan Overview

The study plan is designed to make learning easy, flexible, and engaging for different class types, learning styles, and student levels.

Learning Paths

  • Add ready-made learning paths ordered by recommended sequence
  • Change the order or add custom activities (text, links, multimedia)
  • Drag and drop or click to make changes

Interactive Content

  • Tutorials
  • Try-its (code snippet testing)
  • Exercises
  • Quiz
  • Challenges
  • Labs

Timeline and Pace

Set a timeline for your study plan (e.g., 4, 8, 12, or 24 weeks). You can decide the learning pace and assign different plans to different students to ensure everyone is appropriately challenged.

Track Student Progress

Use analytics to monitor chapter progress, exercise and quiz results, exam outcomes, and more. Challenges can be auto-graded or manually graded, with results accessible to you as the teacher.

End of Pathway Exam

The study plan aligns with the NumPy Certification Exam, which can be taken at the end of the course. The exam summarizes the tutorial and provides detailed student result reports.

Accessibility

All study plans and learning materials are accessible across desktops, tablets, and smartphones, allowing students to learn anytime, anywhere.
