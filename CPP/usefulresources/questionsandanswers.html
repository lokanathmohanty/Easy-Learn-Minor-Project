C++ Interview & Test Preparation Resources 🧑‍💻🎓

This section outlines various formats of questions and tests designed to help individuals prepare for **C++ Certification Exams** and **Job Interviews**, ensuring comprehensive coverage of the language.

Available Question and Answer Formats

The resources are categorized by format and intended purpose:

Sr.NoResource TypePurpose & Description
1 **C++ Interview Questions** A collection of common interview questions with answers hidden to encourage self-testing and challenging your knowledge before viewing the solution.
2 **C++ Online Quiz** A page featuring **Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)**. It provides immediate feedback: green for correct, red for incorrect, along with explanations.
3 **C++ Online Test** A simulated **real-time online test** with a timer, designed to mimic certification exam conditions. It allows you to check your score and performance ranking.
4 **C++ Mock Test** Offline test packages available for download. These mock tests are supplied with an answer key for self-grading and verification of the final score.

These formats collectively cater to different stages of preparation, from initial knowledge assessment (Quiz/Mock Test) to final interview readiness (Interview Questions) and timed exam practice (Online Test).
