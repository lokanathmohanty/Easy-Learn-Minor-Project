Power BI: Sharing Dashboards

Dashboards in Power BI are centralized visualizations that provide a comprehensive overview of key metrics and performance indicators. They combine data from multiple reports and sources, offering tailored views for various roles within an organization.

Dashboards help explore data quickly and make informed decisions. Power BI offers features like Quick Insights to automatically generate data visualizations and summaries from datasets.

Creating Dashboards

  1. In Power BI Service, pin visuals from reports by selecting the pin icon on any chart or graph.
  2. Choose to pin to an existing dashboard or create a new one.
  3. Access dashboards from the My Workspace section to review or customize.

Sharing Dashboards

Power BI allows sharing dashboards with others in your organization, facilitating collaboration and transparency.

  1. Open the dashboard in Power BI Service.
  2. Click the Share option at the top right.
  3. Enter the email addresses of recipients; only Power BI Pro users can share or receive shared dashboards.
  4. Optionally, allow recipients to forward the dashboard or notify them via email.
  5. Recipients can access the dashboard through a direct link or notification.

Sharing dashboards ensures decision-makers have timely access to critical business insights.

Key Dashboard Components and Types

Dashboards can be customized per department or role, such as Finance, Marketing, or Sales, showing KPIs relevant to each audience.

  • Sales Production Dashboard
  • Human Resource Analytics Dashboard
  • Customer Analytics Dashboard

Customization aligns insights with the responsibilities and goals of different user groups.
